# Letter 1 — AeroPay

**To:** support@aeropay.com
**Subject:** Account disabled — unable to deposit or withdraw on PrizePicks

Send this first. It tests the most likely cause and it is the one PrizePicks
support cannot fix for you.

---

Hello,

I am unable to either deposit to or withdraw from my PrizePicks account. Because
both directions are blocked at once, I believe the problem is with my AeroPay
account rather than with PrizePicks.

Account details:

- Name: Kiley Bedwell
- Email on the PrizePicks account: kileybedwell@gmail.com
- Bank account linked: [BANK NAME], ending [LAST 4 ONLY]
- Approximate date the problem began: [DATE]
- Error message shown: "[EXACT TEXT]"

I am asking for three specific things:

1. Confirmation of my AeroPay account status — specifically whether it is
   flagged, disabled, or otherwise restricted.
2. If it is disabled, the reason, and confirmation of any balance you show as
   owed by me, including the date and amount of the transaction that caused it.
3. The steps required to reactivate it, and the timeframe.

If there is a returned or reversed deposit on file, please tell me the amount so
I can settle it immediately. I would rather resolve this than have it sit.

I have [$AMOUNT] in my PrizePicks balance that I cannot access, so I would
appreciate a response within three business days. Please reply in writing to
this email address.

Thank you,
Kiley Bedwell

---

**Do not include:** full account or card numbers, your online banking login, or
photographs of a debit card. Last four digits only. No legitimate support agent
needs more than that, and a request for more is a red flag.
