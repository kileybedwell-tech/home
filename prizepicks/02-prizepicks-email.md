# Letter 2 — PrizePicks

**To:** PrizePicks support (use the email/ticket form in the Help Center, not the
chat widget — you want a timestamped written record)
**Subject:** Account unable to deposit or withdraw — requesting written reason

Send this at the same time as Letter 1. It tests the other two possible causes.

---

Hello,

My account is currently unable to complete either a deposit or a withdrawal.
I am asking for a written explanation of the account's status.

Account details:

- Name: Kiley Bedwell
- Email on the account: kileybedwell@gmail.com
- Approximate date the problem began: [DATE]
- Current balance: [$AMOUNT]
- Error message on withdrawal: "[EXACT TEXT]"
- Error message on deposit: "[EXACT TEXT]"

Please answer each of these directly:

1. Is my account under any restriction, hold, or review? If so, what kind, when
   was it applied, and what is required to lift it?
2. Is my identity verification fully complete? If not, tell me exactly which
   document or field is outstanding and I will supply it today.
3. Is there any playthrough or bonus requirement outstanding on my balance? If
   so, what is the remaining amount?
4. Is my account eligible in my state, and has that eligibility changed?

I have already contacted AeroPay separately regarding the possibility that the
issue is on the payment-processor side.

To be clear about what I want: I want access to my balance of [$AMOUNT]. If the
account cannot be restored to normal use, I am asking that the balance be
returned to me by whatever method you can process.

Please respond in writing within five business days. If I do not receive a
substantive response, I will file a complaint with the Consumer Financial
Protection Bureau and with the applicable state authority.

Thank you,
Kiley Bedwell

---

**Note on that last paragraph:** it is not a bluff and it should not be dropped.
A stated, dated escalation deadline is what moves a ticket out of the automated
queue. Keep it factual and unemotional — no threats beyond naming the regulator
you will actually contact.
